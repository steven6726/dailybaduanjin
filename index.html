Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Our Commitment

Daily Baduanjin ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. Our App operates entirely on your device. Specifically:

  • No personal information — We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • No account required — You can use all features without creating an account or logging in.
  • No device identifiers — We do not collect device IDs, advertising IDs, or any other device-level identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking — We do not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or tracking technologies.

Data Stored Locally

All data generated by your use of the App is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Practice records (exercise duration, scores, check-in history)
  • App preferences (reminder settings, privacy consent status)

This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the App, all data is permanently deleted.

In-App Purchases

To validate the status of your subscription and ensure you receive uninterrupted access to premium features, the App may send your purchase receipt to Apple's App Store servers for verification. This is done solely for functional purposes and does not involve the collection or transmission of any personal information.

Camera Usage

The App uses your device camera for AI-powered pose detection during exercise sessions. Camera frames are processed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit. No video, images, or camera data are saved, stored, or transmitted off your device.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google ML Kit for on-device pose detection. ML Kit processes camera data locally on your device and does not send data to Google servers. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our end. All your data is under your control on your device. You can delete your local data at any time within the App's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the App and updating the "Last updated" date.
